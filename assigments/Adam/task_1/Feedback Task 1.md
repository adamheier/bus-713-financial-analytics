**1. Returns & Visualisation (CLO1, CLO2) — High Distinction**  
Opens by framing the companies and the index clearly, which sets the whole presentation up well. The initial price graphs are a bit compressed, limiting how much comes through. The growth-of-$100 indexed chart is a nice touch, though the choice of 2005 as the starting point needed more explicit justification — that said, it does allow a genuinely interesting "tale of two decades" narrative to emerge from the data. Monthly returns chosen deliberately over daily specifically to reduce noise and get a clearer read on volatility, and this choice was explained rather than just made.

**2. Volatility & Risk (CLO1, CLO2) — High Distinction**  
The combined return-and-volatility bar chart is a strong touch — would have liked more discussion of what "annualised" actually means in context here (30-50% annualised returns showing up on the chart needs unpacking for a non-technical viewer to make sense of). Clustering evidence is thorough and well presented.

**3. VaR/ES (CLO2, CLO3) — High Distinction**  
Very well done. The overlay of VaR and ES on the same chart, each stock above the other, is excellent — makes their relative position immediately clear rather than requiring the viewer to cross-reference separate figures.

**4. CAGR & Inflation (CLO2, CLO3) — High Distinction**  
Does exactly what's needed, well executed. The rebasing into actual dollar values — what the investment was really worth — is a strong communication choice that lands the inflation point clearly.

**5. R Code, Video, Exec Summary (CLO3, CLO4) — Distinction**  
Code is strong and tight, clearly benefiting from working properly with AI assistance to get structure and syntax right rather than just generating and pasting. Went above and beyond with Sharpe and Sortino ratios — presented as tables rather than graphs, which would have landed better visually, but not a major issue. The final summary chart sat rather plainly in the console, but was still talked through effectively.

**Overall:** Very close to a model answer, if not adjacent to one. Every analytical criterion is excellent, and the presentation choices — dollar rebasing, VaR/ES overlay, deliberate return frequency — show real thought about what actually communicates to an investor. The main opportunity left on the table is going a little deeper into the individual story of each company over time — a quibble rather than a real gap.
